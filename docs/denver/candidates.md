addresses, hours and phone numbers especially as leads to check.

## Candidates

### 1. Teocalli Cocina (LoHi)
- **Address:** 1575 Boulder St, Unit C · (303) 479-3123
- **Claimed:** dedicated; 100% GF Mexican kitchen, corn tortillas throughout (FMGF, GlutenScout, Westword)
- **Precautions claimed:** dedicated kitchen; dedicated fryer; knowledgeable staff (FMGF reviews)
- **Dishes:** tacos, tortilla chips, churros
- **Sources:** teocallicocina.com/lohi · [Westword, 28 Jul 2025](https://www.westword.com/food-drink/teocalli-cocina-opens-fourth-restaurant-in-denvers-lohi-25099551/) · [5280](https://5280.com/teocalli-cocina-takes-on-lohi-with-newest-outpost/) · [FMGF](https://www.findmeglutenfree.com/biz/teocalli-cocina/6068257079820288) · [The Celiac MD](https://theceliacmd.com/location/teocalli-cocina-lo-hi/)
- **Open-status risk:** low–moderate; opened July 2025 in the former Post Chicken & Beer space
- **Confidence:** high

### 2. Just BE Kitchen (LoHi)
- **Address:** 2364 15th St
- **Claimed:** dedicated; "100% gluten-free and paleo … fully dedicated gluten-free kitchen" (303 Magazine, Jan 2026); Westword Readers' Choice Best Gluten-Free Restaurant 2025
- **Precautions claimed:** dedicated kitchen; dedicated fryer (list derived from FMGF)
- **Dishes:** breakfast burrito with pork green chile, cauliflower wings, almond-flour French toast
- **Sources:** justbekitchen.com · [303 Magazine](https://303magazine.com/2026/01/a-neighborhood-guide-to-denvers-best-vegan-gluten-free-spots/) · [Westword](https://www.westword.com/best-of-denver/2025/readers-choice/best-gluten-free-restaurant-24106577/) · [Yelp](https://www.yelp.com/biz/just-be-kitchen-lohi-denver)
- **Open-status risk:** LoHi appears open. **The DTC/Greenwood Village location is reported closed**; don't add it.
- **Confidence:** high
